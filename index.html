What the data suggests

    Caveats

    • This shows correlation, not causation. There is no control group of non-participating customers, so organic BAIP growth in the region is also part of these numbers.
    • Enterprise Agreements make up ~77% of cACV. That consumption comes from contracts already in place. The most direct tournament signals are new solution areas and customers who started consuming after the event.
    • The 2024 edition has no "before" period. The data starts in Aug 2024, the same month as that tournament.
    • Vale carries a lot of weight (~42% of the total). Turn on "Exclude Vale" to see results without it.
